============================

Some source in RefractionMap.cs is adapted from the open-source project
liquid-glass-react.

Project: liquid-glass-react
Author: fivepointseven

License: MIT License
Project: https://github.com/rdev/liquid-glass-react

The C# implementation adapts the SmoothStep and rounded-box signed-distance
field ideas from src/shader-utils.ts and src/index.tsx. The original is
JavaScript; this implementation was rewritten in C# and uses floating-point
coordinates. The production renderer currently does not invoke refraction;
the adapted helper remains in the source tree for provenance/compatibility.
